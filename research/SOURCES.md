# Sources and limitations

Moonlight Bajaj 1,239-part catalogue, official owner's manual PDFs, and OEM fiche pages were **not present** in this workspace. No invented OEM part numbers are stored.

## Used (public)

- Bajaj Auto Dominar 250 product literature: 248.77 cc liquid-cooled SOHC 4-valve single, 6-speed, twin-spar frame, USD forks, 100/80-17 and 130/70-17, 300/230 mm discs, dual-channel ABS, 13 L tank, 1453 mm wheelbase, LED twin projector.
- Public photography for silhouette: underbelly exhaust, tank hump, mid-fairings, grab rail.

## Not used (unavailable here)

- Moonlight Bajaj parts catalogue (1,239 parts / 59 diagrams).
- Official Bajaj Dominar 250 owner's manual PDF.
- Official OEM exploded fiches with factory part numbers.

## Modelling policy

Meshes are schematic primitives scaled to published wheelbase, tyre and disc figures. Internal engine parts are educational cutaways. Workshop IDs (for example `DH2-…` were **removed**; UI shows `Not published in this build`).
