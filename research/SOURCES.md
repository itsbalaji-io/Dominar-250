# Sources and limitations

No invented OEM part numbers are stored. Full factory CAD and the owner's-manual PDF are still not in this workspace.

## Used (public)

- Bajaj Auto Dominar 250 product literature: 248.77 cc liquid-cooled SOHC 4-valve single, 6-speed, twin-spar frame, USD forks, 100/80-17 and 130/70-17, 300/230 mm discs, dual-channel ABS, 13 L tank, 1453 mm wheelbase, LED twin projector.
- Official Bajaj product photography (Canyon Red) for silhouette, visor/cowl, tank hump, USD forks, and two-tone body.
- Moonlight Bajaj public fiche https://moonlightbajaj.com/en/catalogue/bajaj/dominar-250-bs-vi-rev-10 — ColorDoc 71120159, Rev. 10, 2025-01-01. Ingested 59 plates, 1,238 line items (site banner 1,239), 17 kits. OEM numbers in the fiche browser are copied from that page.
- Local plate PNGs under `research/refs/` for silhouette work.

## Not used (unavailable here)

- Factory 3D CAD / photogrammetry of 1,239 unique solids.
- Official Bajaj Dominar 250 owner's manual PDF.

## Modelling policy

Meshes are schematic primitives scaled to published wheelbase, tyre and disc figures. Internal engine parts are educational cutaways. The 3D inventory is 171 selectable meshes mapped onto fiche plates; it is not 1,239 unique CAD parts. Workshop IDs stay schematic. Fiche OEM numbers appear only in the plate browser, copied from Moonlight.
